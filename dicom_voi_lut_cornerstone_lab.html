2. VOI LUT Mathematics
DICOM-defined linear VOI transformation, with the half-value terms retained exactly:
y = ((x − (WC − 0.5)) / (WW − 1) + 0.5), then clip to [0,1]
Sigmoid VOI LUT (DICOM PS3.3 C.11.2.1.3):
y = 1 / (1 + exp(−4 × (x − WC) / WW))
x is the modality-value domain after any required Modality LUT/rescale operation. y is normalized here to 0…1 for visualization; a Presentation LUT/display mapping can follow in a full DICOM pipeline.
